# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 20, 2026

## Introduction

aulycmail is a desktop e-mail application. This Privacy Policy explains how the
application handles your information.

**Key point:** aulycmail does not operate servers that collect, store, or process
your mail data. Your information stays on your device except when the
application communicates directly with the mail providers you configure.

## Information We Do Not Collect

aulycmail:

- Does not collect personal information on external servers
- Does not transmit your e-mails, contacts, or credentials to us
- Does not use analytics, telemetry, or advertising tracking
- Does not display advertisements
- Does not sell or share user data

## Information Stored Locally

aulycmail stores these items locally on your device:

- E-mail messages synchronized from configured mail accounts
- Folder metadata and local search indexes
- Attachments cached or saved by the user
- Draft messages
- Mail account configuration
- Passwords or app-specific passwords in the system keychain
- Contacts you create or addresses collected locally from sent mail
- Application settings such as theme, window state, and preferences

## Data Security

Your data security depends on your device security, the system keychain, and the
TLS/SSL connections used when communicating with your configured mail providers.
aulycmail does not provide cloud storage or remote backup.

## Third-Party Services

aulycmail connects only to services you configure, such as your IMAP/SMTP mail
provider. Your use of those providers is governed by their own terms and
privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. Removing the application may
leave local data in the application support directory. To fully remove local
mail data, delete the aulycmail data directory after uninstalling the
application.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current policy is published at
https://aulyc.com/aulycmail/privacy.
